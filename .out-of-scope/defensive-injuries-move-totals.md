# Defensive injuries move totals (#219)

**Rejected 2026-09-11.** A hypothesis that a defensive downgrade adds to the total while
cancelling on the spread. Its treatment — "a defensive injury" — has no pre-registrable
definition: which positions, how many snaps, ruled out or questionable, defined from the
injury report or from the snap counts after the fact. `docs/method.md` rule 1 says the
decision rule comes before the numbers; without a treatment definition there is no rule to
fix. The one public survey the ticket cites found the spread effect essentially zero and
never checked totals, which is a reason to be curious, not a design.

Reopen with: a treatment definition a script can apply before kickoff, the outcome window
it is scored on, and the MDE at the sample the archive holds.
